Flock, a Magic Eye

Tessellation 15, warped around a hidden gull. Relax your eyes and look past the screen.

How to see it. Leave "Fit to window" off so one image pixel is one screen pixel. Sit about an arm's length away, look at the two dots, and let your focus drift behind the screen until they become three. Hold that, then look around: a gull with its wings spread floats toward you.

Choosing the repeat. If it strains, make the repeat smaller. Most people are comfortable at 35 to 50 mm on screen. On a 24-inch 1080p monitor, 150 px is about 42 mm.
